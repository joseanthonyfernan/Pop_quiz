=========================================
      POP QUIZ SYSTEM GUIDELINES
=========================================

1. ATTENDANCE POLICY
--------------------
Attendance is recorded automatically upon login based on the following schedule:
- PRESENT : Logins on or before 09:30 AM.
- LATE    : Logins between 09:31 AM and 01:30 PM.
- ABSENT  : Logins after 01:31 PM.

2. LOG-OUT POLICY
-----------------
Log-outs (Time-Outs) are automatically recorded at fixed intervals:
- Morning Session: 12:45 PM (for logins before 12:45 PM)
- Afternoon Session: 05:45 PM (for logins after 12:45 PM)

3. QUIZ GUIDELINES
------------------
- QUIZ AVAILABILITY: Quizzes are scheduled and may be released at 
  specific dates or times.
- PROGRESS TRACKING: Your progress is automatically saved. You can 
  see your completed questions and point totals on your dashboard.
- COMPLETION: A quiz is considered finished when all questions in the 
  current set are answered.
- TIME LIMITS: Pay attention to any countdown timers if applicable for 
  specific quizzes.

4. STUDENT PROCEDURES
---------------------
- LOGGING IN: Enter your Student Number to log in. First-time users 
  will be prompted to complete their profile.
- TAKING QUIZZES: Navigate to the "Take Quiz" section from your 
  dashboard.
- VIEWING RESULTS: Your scores and top performers are visible on your 
  dashboard and the results page.
- LOGGING OUT: The system automatically handles your attendance log-out. 
  Just ensure you've completed your activities before the system logout times.

5. ADMIN PROCEDURES
-------------------
- MANAGING QUIZZES: Admins can create, edit, and schedule quizzes 
  through the Admin Dashboard.
- MONITORING ATTENDANCE: Real-time attendance tracking (Time-In and 
  Time-Out) is available in the Admin Attendance section.
- RESULTS & ANALYTICS: Admins can view comprehensive results, top 
  performers, and student progress.

6. TECHNICAL SUPPORT
--------------------
In case of issues with login, quiz submission, or timer malfunctions, 
please contact the system administrator immediately.
